---
name: creative
use-when: the output is an ARTIFACT that must LAND on an AUDIENCE — a deck, long-form writing/docs, a design, marketing
truth-source: a human audience
---

- **consumer:** the human audience — readers, viewers, attendees, users of the doc.
- **the-real-thing:** the artifact **as the audience receives it** — the rendered deck, the published doc, the final
  design — not the outline, the draft, or the source file.
- **verify:** experience it the way the audience will — read/watch it start to end in its delivered form; check that it
  *lands* (clear, coherent, on-message, right length/level); and **fact-check every figure, quote, and claim** in it, so
  the polish isn't riding on something false.
- **evidence:** a walkthrough of the delivered artifact (the actual slides/pages as shown) plus a fact-check log of its
  claims and numbers — not "I wrote the content" and not a screenshot of the editor.
- **verify anti-patterns:** shipping the outline as if it were the deck; never viewing it rendered; unchecked figures
  presented with confidence; "it reads well to me" with no pass for the actual audience/level.
