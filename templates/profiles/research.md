---
name: research
use-when: the output is CLAIMS that must be TRUE TO SOURCES — literature reviews, market/competitive, investigations, compliance
truth-source: external sources / prior art
---

- **consumer:** someone who will make a decision based on the findings.
- **the-real-thing:** the findings/claims, each **traceable to a primary source** — not a confident synthesis floating
  free of provenance.
- **verify:** re-check each load-bearing claim against its **primary source** (not memory, not a secondary summary, not
  another model's paraphrase); confirm the source actually says what the claim asserts, and note where it doesn't.
- **evidence:** a citation/provenance ledger — *claim → source → quote or locator* — with any claim you could not
  ground flagged as unverified rather than quietly dropped or asserted anyway.
- **verify anti-patterns:** citing a source you didn't open; relaying a sub-agent's finding as fact without checking it;
  presenting a plausible synthesis as established; a dead/hallucinated link.
